AgriFoodData model

Machine-readable specification of the data model for digital agriculture and farm management systems: 47 entities in 14 packages, bound to the classes and properties of the agrifooddata ontology.

The machine-readable specification of the data model of the Recommendation Data model for digital agriculture and farm management systems (draft, revision 3, proposed to ITU-T Study Group 20, 2026-07-29): its entities, attributes and relationships, the rules and state models they obey, and a REST binding — as OpenAPI, JSON Schema, JSON-LD and SHACL, bound to the agrifooddata ontology.
