Farm

P2 Farm and Sites · Annex A.7 · agfoda:Farm · JSON Schema · /farms

The Farm entity shall represent a single agricultural operation or managed agricultural unit. It is the main container for fields, sites and related operational data and is the primary entity for assigning access rights.
