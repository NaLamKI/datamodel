Stand

P4 Cropping Planning and Cultivation · Annex A.13 · agfoda:Stand · JSON Schema · /stands

The Stand entity shall represent a perennial planting, such as a vineyard, an orchard, a hop garden or an asparagus planting, whose life cycle extends over several years and is therefore distinct from the seasonal CultivationPeriod.

Constraints

  • clearingDate is not earlier than plantingDate (V08).

In addition, for every entity: createdByUserId is set where originType is USER, createdByJobId where it is SERVICE (V04).
