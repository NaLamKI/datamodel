Certification

P11 Compliance and Audit · Annex A.39 · agfoda:Certification · JSON Schema · /certifications

The Certification entity shall record a certification held by the holding.

Constraints

  • validTo is not earlier than validFrom (V01).

In addition, for every entity: createdByUserId is set where originType is USER, createdByJobId where it is SERVICE (V04).
