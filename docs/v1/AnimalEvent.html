AnimalEvent

P8 Animal Husbandry · Annex A.27 · agfoda:AnimalEvent · JSON Schema · /animal-events

The AnimalEvent entity shall record an occurrence relating to an individual animal or to a group. The type of occurrence is given by eventTypeUri and may denote, among others, birth, insemination, pregnancy diagnosis, parturition, drying off, weighing, milk recording, vaccination, treatment, diagnosis, hoof care, relocation, entry, departure or death.

Constraints

  • Exactly one of animalId, animalGroupId is set (V03).

In addition, for every entity: createdByUserId is set where originType is USER, createdByJobId where it is SERVICE (V04).
