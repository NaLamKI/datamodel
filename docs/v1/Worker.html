Worker

P1 Party, Users and Access · Annex A.3 · agfoda:Worker · JSON Schema · /workers

The Worker entity shall represent a person who performs work on the holding, whether or not that person has a system account. It carries the evidence of qualification that may be required for regulated operations, such as a certificate of competence for the application of plant protection products.

Constraints

  • endsAt is not earlier than startsAt (V01).

In addition, for every entity: createdByUserId is set where originType is USER, createdByJobId where it is SERVICE (V04).
