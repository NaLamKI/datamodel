Clauses 12 and 13 of the Recommendation

Where each rule is checked: JSON Schema and SHACL on a single record, SHACL dataset on a dataset holding the records referred to and the concept schemes, interface only in the persistence or interface layer, because the rule needs history, sums over many records, geometry or time. A rule expressed with “should” raises a warning.

PENDING → RUNNING, RUNNING → SUCCEEDED, RUNNING → FAILED, PENDING → CANCELLED, RUNNING → CANCELLED. Terminal: SUCCEEDED, FAILED, CANCELLED.

RegulatoryReport.status

DRAFT → GENERATED, GENERATED → SUBMITTED, SUBMITTED → ACCEPTED, SUBMITTED → REJECTED, REJECTED → GENERATED, GENERATED → DRAFT. Terminal: ACCEPTED.

A rejected report is either regenerated or corrected by a new report referencing the earlier one through supersedesReportId. From SUBMITTED onwards the report is otherwise immutable (rule V15).

Derived views

Stock level (clauses 6, 7.5 and 8.6)

The sum of the StockMovement records for a combination of productId, lotId and storage location.

A movement adds its quantity to the level at toStorageLocationId and subtracts it from the level at fromStorageLocationId, per productId, lotId and unit. A movement without a storage location on one side does not affect a level on that side. Levels in different units are not added up.

Gross margin (clause 8.10)

For a CultivationPeriod or an AnimalGroup: the sum of the Economics records associated with output quantities, less the sum of those associated with input quantities and with the machine and labour resources deployed in the corresponding operations. Where an Economics record is absent, the imputed rates Machine.costRatePerHour and Worker.costRatePerHour, multiplied by the duration recorded in ActivityResource, should be used instead.

Nutrient balance (clause 7.5)

Derived from InputOutput records together with Product.composition.

Animal performance indicators (clauses 7.5 and 8.8)

Lactation yield, calving interval, daily weight gain, replacement rate and the like, derived from AnimalEvent and Observation records.

Crop rotation (clause 7.4)

The temporal sequence of CroppingPlanEntry and CultivationPeriod records for a given Region across harvest years. Rules governing the sequence are validation logic applied to it, not structures of the model.
