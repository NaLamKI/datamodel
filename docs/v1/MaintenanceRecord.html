MaintenanceRecord

P7 Machinery and Equipment · Annex A.24 · agfoda:MaintenanceRecord · JSON Schema · /maintenance-records

The MaintenanceRecord entity shall record maintenance, inspection or repair of a machine.

Attributes

AttributeTypeDescriptionOntologyValues
