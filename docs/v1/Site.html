Site

P2 Farm and Sites · Annex A.8 · agfoda:Site · JSON Schema · /sites

The Site entity shall represent a built or technical location of the holding, such as a barn, a greenhouse compartment, a storage building, a silo, a farmyard, a biogas plant or a machinery shed. It complements the field-centred spatial structure and carries animal housing, protected cultivation and storage locations.

Constraints

In addition, for every entity: createdByUserId is set where originType is USER, createdByJobId where it is SERVICE (V04).
