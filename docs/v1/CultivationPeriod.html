CultivationPeriod

P4 Cropping Planning and Cultivation · Annex A.14 · agfoda:CultivationPeriod · JSON Schema · /cultivation-periods

The CultivationPeriod entity shall represent the cultivation of a specific crop, including its cultivar, on a specific Region over a defined period of time. The period is not restricted to a single growing season: depending on crop type, cultivar, environmental conditions and management practice, it may cover part of a season, a full season, or several successive or overlapping cultivation cycles. Where cultivation cycles overlap in time on the same region, each cycle shall be represented by a separate CultivationPeriod instance. For a perennial planting it represents the annual vegetative cycle of a Stand.

Constraints

  • endsAt is not earlier than startsAt (V01).

In addition, for every entity: createdByUserId is set where originType is USER, createdByJobId where it is SERVICE (V04).
