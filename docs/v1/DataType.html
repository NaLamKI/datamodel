DataType

P9 Observations and Data · Annex A.33 · agfoda:DataType · JSON Schema · /data-types

The DataType entity shall provide an extensible categorization of datasets.

Attributes

AttributeTypeDescriptionOntologyValues
id (EntityBase)UUIDMUnique identifier of the record, its primary key
