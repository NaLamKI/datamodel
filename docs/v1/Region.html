Region

P3 Fields and Geospatial Data · Annex A.10 · agfoda:Region · JSON Schema · /regions

The Region entity shall represent a specific, geographically defined area within a Field. It permits the subdivision of a field into management zones, experimental plots or areas with particular characteristics, and is the primary spatial unit to which cultivation information, observations and data are attached.

Constraints

  • endsAt is not earlier than startsAt (V01).

In addition, for every entity: createdByUserId is set where originType is USER, createdByJobId where it is SERVICE (V04).

Unique

  • Exactly one default Region per fieldId (rule V05).
