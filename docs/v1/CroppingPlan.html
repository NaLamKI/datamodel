CroppingPlan

P4 Cropping Planning and Cultivation · Annex A.11 · agfoda:CroppingPlan · JSON Schema · /cropping-plans

The CroppingPlan entity shall represent a versioned cropping plan for a harvest year, permitting several alternative plans to be held in parallel.
