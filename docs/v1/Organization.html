Organization

P1 Party, Users and Access · Annex A.1 · agfoda:Organization · JSON Schema · /organizations

The Organization entity shall represent a legal entity, such as a farming business, a cooperative, a contracting business, an advisory service, a laboratory, a veterinary practice, a trading partner, a certification body or a public authority. Within a given deployment it is the top-level container for all other data; in a federated environment each participating organization maintains its own Organization root.
