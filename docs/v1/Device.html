Device

P9 Observations and Data · Annex A.30 · agfoda:Device · JSON Schema · /devices

The Device entity shall represent a sensor, an item of equipment or a station as a Thing in the sense of [W3C WoT-TD].

Constraints

  • At most one of siteId, regionId, machineId, animalId is set (clause 8.9).

In addition, for every entity: createdByUserId is set where originType is USER, createdByJobId where it is SERVICE (V04).
