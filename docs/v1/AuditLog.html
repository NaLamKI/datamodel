AuditLog

P11 Compliance and Audit · Annex A.40 · agfoda:AuditLog · JSON Schema · /audit-logs

The AuditLog entity shall record changes to the records of the model. The audit log shall be persisted in an append-only manner and should be exportable in accordance with W3C PROV-O.

Written by the system on every change, append-only; the interface offers it for reading only.
