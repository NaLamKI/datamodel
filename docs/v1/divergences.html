Divergences

Where the Recommendation, the ontology and this specification part ways

The Recommendation takes precedence (clause 8). A difference is named here so that it is fixed where it belongs, instead of being papered over in a generator. The ontology is checked as of 2026-09-27, commit 809c186ecfb577ae6ba71b42a2d3717d639cb020.

Ontology

SubjectFindingResolution
O10Clause 7.2 admits domain-specific vocabularies alongside AGROVOC "provided that the concepts they express are mapped to AGROVOC concepts". The ontology maps its schemes crops and pests in part, in the link set links/agrovoc.ttl, where AGROVOC names a concept as the ontology does; the rest of them, and the stages of phenology, carry no mapping. They are values of CultivationPeriod.typeUri, CroppingPlanEntry.cropUri, Stand.cropUri, Observation.observedPropertyUri and Activity.bbchStage.None here; the remaining mappings belong in the ontology, which lists them as a gap of its own.

Recommendation

SubjectFindingResolution
R1Machine.descriptionClauses 5.1 f) and 9 list Machine among the entities with a self-description; the table in Annex A.23 has no description attribute.Included, optional, holding a Thing Description, with the source noted on the attribute.
R2ProcessingService.typeUriClause 8.12 and Annex B require a typeUri where serviceType is CUSTOM; the table in Annex A.42 has no such attribute.Included, optional; its presence where serviceType is CUSTOM is checked as a "should".
R3Stand.description, Result.descriptionAnnex A.13 describes Stand.description as a Thing Description, although clause 5.1 f) does not list Stand; Annex A.45 describes Result.description merely as a description, although clause 5.1 f) lists Result.Both are read as Thing Descriptions — the reading that follows from either source.
R4RegulatoryReport.statusTable 4 permits REJECTED → GENERATED "where the report is regenerated", while rule V15 makes a report immutable from SUBMITTED onwards and admits corrections only through a new report.The transition is kept as Table 4 lists it; the conflict is for the Recommendation to resolve.
R5Stock levelClauses 7.5 and 8.6 derive stock levels per productId, lotId and storageLocationId, but StockMovement has no storageLocationId — it has fromStorageLocationId and toStorageLocationId.A movement adds to the level at its destination and subtracts from the level at its origin (rules.yaml, derived view stock-level).
R6Budget.scopeType COST_CATEGORYscopeRefId is a UUID "referring to the object identified by scopeType", but a cost category is a concept (costCategoryUri), not a record, and Budget has no attribute to hold it.None; for COST_CATEGORY, scopeRefId has no defined target.
R7Region.areaField carries area and areaUnit, Region only area — its unit is not stated.None.
R8Appendix IIThe Thing Description in Appendix II does not conform to WoT TD 1.1: it lacks the mandatory securityDefinitions and security, its property affordances lack forms, `value` is not a member of a property affordance, the prefix td is not defined, and the AGROVOC namespace is given as a JSON-LD context, which it is not.The example of a Field in examples/ carries a Thing Description that does conform.
R9Appendix IIIFour of the eight AGROVOC identifiers in Appendix III name other concepts: c_5979 is "plant resources" (not ploughing), c_6954 "Selenomonas" (not seeding), c_3533 "Hedysarum coronarium" (not harvesting), c_5192 "nitrogen" (not nitrogen fertilizers). Checked against the AGROVOC service on 2026-09-26.The Recommendation should read c_6021 (ploughing), c_7268 (sowing), c_3500 (harvesting) and c_5195 (nitrogen fertilizers). The ontology already uses c_7268 and c_3500 in activity-types.
R10ISO 3166-1Country codes shall conform to ISO 3166-1 (clause 5.1 j)), which defines alpha-2, alpha-3 and numeric codes; the Recommendation does not say which.All three forms are accepted; alpha-2 is recommended in the descriptions.
R11Lot.supplierOrganizationIdRule V13 requires purchased lots to set supplierOrganizationId, but whether a lot was purchased is not recorded in the lot itself.At most one origin of own production is checked on the record; the supplier requirement is left to the interface layer.
