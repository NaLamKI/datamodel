AnimalGroup

P8 Animal Husbandry · Annex A.26 · agfoda:AnimalGroup · JSON Schema · /animal-groups

The AnimalGroup entity shall represent a herd, a group or the occupancy of a pen as a unit of management. In poultry and pig production it is frequently the only level at which animals are managed, individual animals not being recorded.

Constraints

  • endsAt is not earlier than startsAt (V01).

In addition, for every entity: createdByUserId is set where originType is USER, createdByJobId where it is SERVICE (V04).
