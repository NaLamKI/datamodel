Product

P6 Products and Inventory · Annex A.19 · agfoda:Product · JSON Schema · /products

The Product entity shall represent the master data of an input or of produce.

Constraints

  • registrationNumber is set where category is PLANT_PROTECTION or VETERINARY_MEDICINE (clause 8.6).

In addition, for every entity: createdByUserId is set where originType is USER, createdByJobId where it is SERVICE (V04).

Unique

  • registrationNumber (the authorization scheme concerned).

Referenced by

Lot.productId, StockMovement.productId, AnimalEvent.productId, FeedRationComponent.productId, InputOutput.productId
