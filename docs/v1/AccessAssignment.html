AccessAssignment

P1 Party, Users and Access · Annex A.6 · agfoda:AccessAssignment · JSON Schema · /access-assignments

The AccessAssignment entity shall assign a Role to a User for a specific Farm, and may limit that assignment in time. It realizes access to several holdings and the granting of temporary access to advisers and contractors.
